02 / INDEPENDENT RESEARCHCOAUTHORED MANUSCRIPT · UNDER REVIEW

When are knee-pain
predictions less reliable?

Structure-Pain Discordance and 48-Month Knee Pain Prediction Error in the Osteoarthritis Initiative

With Yuheng Tang, I studied whether disagreement between knee X-ray severity and reported pain tracks errors in future pain prediction. I directed the study, contributed to methodology and clinical interpretation, integrated analyses, and wrote the manuscript. Tang led software and numerical modeling.

WHAT THE FULL RESULT SHOWS

The unadjusted discordance–error association did not persist after baseline-pain adjustment in a post-freeze sensitivity analysis. The adjusted coefficient was −0.011 (95% CI −0.043 to 0.022). Symptom burden is the stronger candidate explanation in these data. The work is internally validated research, not a clinical prediction service.
