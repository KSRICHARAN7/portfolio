When ECG filtering drops the cases that matter

Waveform Quality, Confidence, and AF-Case Retention in Single Lead ECG Selective Classification: A Controlled Noise Benchmark

This study tested whether waveform quality helped identify classification errors under recorded ECG artifact. The primary comparison did not establish superiority. At 90% overall coverage, the combined filter retained only 9.27–48.34% of AF-positive records across the four primary noise conditions.

That is the central lesson: keeping most records does not mean keeping most AF cases. The favorable quality-only ablation remains exploratory.

15,292 ECG records from 13,796 participants; single lead II extracted from PTB-XL. Controlled noise benchmark, not a clinical or wearable validation. Unpublished and not peer reviewed.
