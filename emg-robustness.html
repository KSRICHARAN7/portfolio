The experiment

I kept each person’s trained decoder, filter settings, windows, and scoring events fixed. For each missing channel, I replaced its three features with that person’s training means. This tests a known missing channel with simple imputation; it does not test automatic fault detection or physical electrode failures.

The extension plan was committed before this run. The original DB5 results were already known, so this is an exploratory analysis on previously seen data. It is separate from the original manuscript and has no new participants.

How I measured the loss

For each person, I averaged correct active-interior output across the 16 single-channel masks, then subtracted that average from their intact result. The primary estimate is the mean of those eight losses for the asymmetric filter.

The interval resamples people as paired units: 10,000 draws, seed 5015. Channels and frames are not independent participants. All eight people’s averaged losses were positive. This descriptive interval does not establish generalization to a new cohort.

What this adds

A reproducible stress test of the existing pipeline, with a plan, a fixed analysis, and an audit trail. The intact commands exactly matched archived outputs for all eight people; all 320 evaluations retained 34 scoring events per person.

Limits: one dataset, eight people, offline intact-limb recordings, known masks, and one imputation rule. No DB2 extension, retraining, hardware validation, or closed-loop user trial was run.
