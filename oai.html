Independent research with Yuheng Tang

When are knee-pain
predictions less reliable?

Manuscript under review

We tested whether a mismatch between knee X-rays and reported pain tracked errors in future pain predictions.

Using the Osteoarthritis Initiative, we evaluated 48-month predictions with participant-grouped nested cross-validation.

My contribution

I directed the study, contributed to methodology and clinical interpretation, integrated analyses, and wrote the manuscript. Tang led software and numerical modeling.

Read the full manuscript ↗Inspect the code ↗

The result that matters

The association disappeared after adjusting for baseline pain.

The frozen unadjusted coefficient was 0.301. A post-freeze sensitivity analysis gave −0.011 after baseline-pain adjustment (95% CI −0.043 to 0.022).

That changes the interpretation: these data do not establish an independent discordance effect.
