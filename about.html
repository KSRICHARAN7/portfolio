A little more about me

Hi again.

I’m Sricharan, though I usually go by Sri.

I’m a senior at Ardrey Kell High School in Charlotte and a dual-enrolled student at Central Piedmont Community College.

I’m interested in the space between computing and medicine: how we turn messy information into something useful, and how we decide whether it’s reliable enough to trust.

That interest has taken me from knee-pain prediction research to muscle-signal command filtering. Clinical shadowing has also given me a chance to see how different the questions can look outside a dataset.

I co-founded HourQuest because I wanted students to have a place to start volunteering even when getting somewhere or finding a formal opportunity was difficult.

I’m learning through clinical shadowing, team competitions, and an AI/ML mentorship with AppNAI. I like having something concrete to work on, whether that’s a research question or helping a service event run smoothly.
